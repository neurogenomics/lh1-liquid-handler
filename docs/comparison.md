# Comparison with the Opentrons Flex and the Hamilton STARlet

Compiled on 2026-11-24 from the makers' published pages and LH-1's own files. Every LH-1 figure is designed, not measured. The commercial figures are the makers' claims under their own test conditions.

Rows that refer to a specific assay or to a comparison with work by hand are left out. The comparison was compiled before the MVP was designed, so the MVP column is not shown; the MVP's figures are in [mvp.md](mvp.md).

Where the commercial machines are stronger, the table says so: they are on sale, measured, supported and certified, they sense liquid levels, pick up part columns of tips and have published accuracy. LH-1 has none of these yet.
