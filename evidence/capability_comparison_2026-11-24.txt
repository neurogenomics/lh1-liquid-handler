LH-1 CAPABILITY COMPARISON: LH-1 (designed) vs OPENTRONS FLEX vs HAMILTON MICROLAB STARlet
Compiled 24 November 2026. Read-only study; the only files written are this file and its .json twin.

STATUS OF LH-1. Designed and simulated only. Nothing has been built or measured. Every LH-1 figure below is
DESIGNED (CAD, calculation or planner simulation), not measured. The second independent check (24 Nov 2026)
left two open blockers in the tool-exchange latch and coupling and judged the starter not worth building as
designed at the lab's run sizes. LH-1 has had no electrical or machinery safety assessment.

COMMERCIAL FIGURES are the makers' published claims, read on the date given in the source list. Hamilton's own
product-spec web pages returned HTTP 403; Hamilton figures come from the maker's brochure (maker domain) and
the maker's operator manual on a third-party host (flagged). Figures from search summaries or third parties
are flagged SECONDARY. Anything not found is stated as not found.

Columns: LH-1 MVP (first-stage build as defined in the brief; not designed in the project files) | LH-1 starter (size S) |
LH-1 large (size L) | Opentrons Flex | Hamilton STARlet. Source ids refer to the lists at the end.

==============================================================================================================
1. STATUS / EVIDENCE BASE
==============================================================================================================
  LH-1 MVP:
    Defined in the brief only (one carriage, fixed eight-channel 30-300 uL, passive deck, no modules, no
    gripper, no tool exchange). Not designed, built or simulated.
  LH-1 starter:
    Designed and simulated only (CAD and calculation). Nothing built or measured. Second independent check (24
    Nov 2026): two open blockers in the tool-exchange latch and coupling; verdict 'not worth building as
    designed' at the lab's run sizes.
  LH-1 large:
    Designed and simulated only. Layout rule-checked; ADR-011 says the large layout was not interference-
    checked at that date.
  Opentrons Flex:
    Commercial product, on sale; published specifications.
  Hamilton STARlet:
    Commercial product (Microlab STAR line); published specifications.
  Sources: L1, L2, L11, L8; F1; H1
  Note:
    Every LH-1 figure below is DESIGNED (CAD, calculation or planner simulation), not measured.

2. FOOTPRINT W X D X H
==============================================================================================================
  LH-1 MVP:
    Not designed: no CAD, simulation or wiring exists for this configuration in the files read. The frame
    width and height are fixed across the family, so an MVP in the starter frame would be no smaller than the
    starter (inference).
  LH-1 starter:
    1,456 x 894 x 1,190 mm with extraction fan (1,344 wide without). Bench area with clearances 1,706 x 994
    mm; needs a bench at least 894 mm deep. DESIGNED.
  LH-1 large:
    1,456 x 1,114 x 1,190 mm with fan. Bench area 1,706 x 1,214 mm. DESIGNED. (Intermediate M: 1,456 x 1,004 x
    1,190.)
  Opentrons Flex:
    870 x 690 x 840 mm (W, D, H).
  Hamilton STARlet:
    1,124 mm wide (1,387 mm with multi-probe head per brochure; 1,450 per manual) x 781 mm deep (brochure;
    manual says 795; autoload about 1,006-1,011) x 903 mm high.
  Sources: L1, L9; F1; H1, H2
  Note:
    LH-1 figures disagree between ADR-011 (starter 1,292 x 894 x 1,140) and the CAD/ADR-014 (1,456 x 894 or
    897 x 1,190); the CAD is newer. Hamilton's brochure and manual disagree on depth and on width with the
    multi-probe head.

4. DECK POSITIONS
==============================================================================================================
  LH-1 MVP:
    Passive deck: one tip rack, one reservoir, two plates, tip waste (as defined in the brief).
  LH-1 starter:
    18 deck places on a 132 x 110 mm grid in 4 rows, plus a person-loaded 1,000 uL tip rack on the shelf, a
    shelf with tip-supply towers, an 8-bay pipette dock. Several places are dedicated (thermocycler and its
    lid park, cold blocks, tip frames, tip parking, tip and liquid waste, magnet, shaker, plate stack).
    DESIGNED.
  LH-1 large:
    28 deck places in 6 rows (adds a balance and a weighing nest, a second plate stack, extra tube racks, a
    second magnet, two empty places), plus shelf towers and two edge hand-off positions. DESIGNED. (M: 23
    places plus shelf rack.)
  Opentrons Flex:
    12 ANSI/SLAS slots reachable by pipettes, plus 4 staging slots (gripper only).
  Hamilton STARlet:
    30 tracks: up to 5 six-track carriers of 5 plate or tip-rack positions each (25 SBS positions if the whole
    deck is used that way), or up to 30 single-track tube carriers of 24 or 32 tubes. Work area 675 x 465 mm;
    labware up to 140 mm tall.
  Sources: L1, L7, L8; F1, F15; H2
  Note:
    ADR-011 states 16/20/25 places for S/M/L; the CAD reports 18/23/28 deck places. Counting conventions
    differ (shelf rack, edge column).

==============================================================================================================
6. PIPETTING CHANNELS AND VOLUME RANGES
==============================================================================================================
  LH-1 MVP:
    One fixed eight-channel pipette, 30-300 uL (brief).
  LH-1 starter:
    Two tool carriages, each takes any tool. Tool set: eight-channel 0.5-10, 5-50 and 30-300 uL; single-
    channel 0.5-10, 10-100 (bay reserved) and 100-1,000 uL; a 10 mL syringe
    dispense tool (optional, refused by firmware until commissioned). Each pipette tool is a bought hand
    pipette in a machined cradle, plunger pressed by a motor on the carriage. DESIGNED.
  LH-1 large:
    Same head and tool set as the starter (width, head and dock are fixed across sizes). DESIGNED.
  Opentrons Flex:
    Two mounts: any two 1- or 8-channel pipettes (1-50 or 5-1,000 uL each), or one 96-channel (1-200 or
    5-1,000 uL) across both mounts.
  Hamilton STARlet:
    8 independent channels (brochure spec table), 1,000 uL channels covering about 0.5-1,000 uL by tip (10,
    50, 300, 1,000 uL tips) and optional 5 mL channels (50-5,000 uL); optional CO-RE 96 or 384 multi-probe
    head. Independent Y spacing and Z per channel.
  Sources: L2, L7, L1; F2; H1, H2
  Note:
    STARlet maximum channel count beyond 8 was not found in the pages read (the manual covers 4-16 channels
    for the STAR line).

==============================================================================================================
7. AUTOMATIC PIPETTE EXCHANGE
==============================================================================================================
  LH-1 MVP:
    No (brief).
  LH-1 starter:
    Yes, DESIGNED: 8-bay dock, tools passive, kinematic coupling of bought balls, vees and a pull stud, latch
    opened by a dock lift bar. About 92 s per exchange (head report). OPEN PROBLEMS: two blockers in the
    latch/coupling (keyhole alignment during exchange; sideways load on the coupling). Coupling repeatability
    (0.10 mm needed) is unproven.
  LH-1 large:
    Same as starter.
  Opentrons Flex:
    No. The user attaches and removes pipettes with captive screws, then runs a probe calibration. The gripper
    sits on its own extension mount.
  Hamilton STARlet:
    No pipette exchange; channels are fixed. Channels can pick up tools (CO-RE gripper paddles) during a run.
  Sources: L2, L9, L11; F17, F3; H1
  Note:
    The machine-family report gives 52.9-53.5 s per exchange; the head report and ADR-014 give 92.3 s after
    the dock lift was added.

==============================================================================================================
8. PARTIAL-COLUMN TIP PICK-UP
==============================================================================================================
  LH-1 MVP:
    No.
  LH-1 starter:
    No. Picking up fewer than eight tips with an eight-channel tool is not designed in (ADR-012 waiver,
    reopened by ADR-013; off in the planner). Single-well work goes to single-channel tools.
  LH-1 large:
    No (same).
  Opentrons Flex:
    Yes: 8-channel 2-7 consecutive tips or a single tip; 96-channel a column, a row or a single tip; rack must
    sit directly on the deck.
  Hamilton STARlet:
    Inherent: each channel picks up its own tip, so any subset of channels can be used; CO-RE 96/384 heads
    pipette full or partial plates.
  Sources: L10, L11; F2; H1
  Note:
    The second check names part-column pick-up as one condition for LH-1 to match the bench at small runs.

==============================================================================================================
9. STATED ACCURACY AND PRECISION
==============================================================================================================
  LH-1 MVP:
    None measured.
  LH-1 starter:
    None measured. No machine accuracy is claimed. The bought hand pipettes' own specifications do not
    transfer to a motor-driven plunger; bench test CT-6 (accuracy after exchange, by weight) is planned, not
    run.
  LH-1 large:
    None measured.
  Opentrons Flex:
    Published per pipette, e.g. 1-channel 5-1,000 uL: 50 uL CV 0.30 %, +/-0.50 %; 200 uL 0.15 %, +/-0.50 %.
    8-channel 5-1,000 uL: 50 uL 0.60 %, +/-2.50 %; 200 uL 0.25 %, +/-1.00 %; 1,000 uL 0.15 %, +/-0.70 %.
    8-channel 1-50 uL at 1 uL (20 uL tip): CV 6.00 %, +/-10.00 %. Gantry positioning 0.1 mm.
  Hamilton STARlet:
    Published (1,000 uL channel, disposable tips): 1 uL CV 4.0 %, trueness 5.0 %; 10 uL (10 uL tip) 1 %, 1.5
    %; 50 uL (50 uL tip) 0.75 %, 2.0 %; 200 uL (300 uL tip) 0.75 %, 1.0 %; 1,000 uL 0.75 %, 1.0 %; 0.5 uL 6.0
    %, 10.0 %. 'Test criteria available upon request.' x-y-z positional accuracy 0.1 mm. Each instrument ships
    with a Declaration of Quality of its final-inspection pipetting results.
  Sources: L2, L10, L11; F2, F15; H2
  Note:
    Commercial figures are the makers' own claims under their test conditions.

==============================================================================================================
10. LIQUID-LEVEL SENSING AND PIPETTING VERIFICATION
==============================================================================================================
  LH-1 MVP:
    None defined.
  LH-1 starter:
    No pressure or capacitive sensing (the hand pipettes have none). Planned checks, all DESIGNED and
    unproven: through-beam tip gate; side camera for tips on, straight and liquid in tip; head camera reading
    deck markers; overview camera recording runs. Checks can only hold a run. A dock bay and cable are
    reserved for a future bought pressure-sensing channel. No weighing in the starter.
  LH-1 large:
    As starter, plus a weighing nest and a 0.1 mg balance on the deck (dispense checked by weight where
    resolvable; ADR-005 expects a few mg, i.e. roughly 5-10 uL and above).
  Opentrons Flex:
    Pressure sensing for liquid detection: one sensor on 1-channel; channels 1 and 8 only on 8-channel; A1 and
    H12 only on 96-channel. Capacitance (contact) sensors on the same channels. Optical tip-attachment sensor
    on all pipettes. One 2 MP deck camera for recording.
  Hamilton STARlet:
    cLLD (conductive liquids, needs conductive tips) and pLLD (all liquids, individual channels only); TADM
    per-channel pressure curves against tolerance bands in real time (software option); MAD (detects clots,
    empty wells); Anti-Droplet Control for volatile liquids; capacitance-based clot detection.
  Sources: L4, L5, L7, L8; F2, F15; H1, H2

  Opentrons Flex:
    96-tip racks in deck slots and staging slots; Stacker holds up to 6 tip racks each, up to 4 Stackers per
    Flex. Trash bin or waste chute fixture.
  Hamilton STARlet:
    Tip racks on carriers (5 per 6-track carrier); tip waste on deck; autoload option loads carriers and reads
    barcodes. No dedicated tip stacker found in the pages read.
  Sources: L1; F9, F18, F15; H1, H2
  Note:
    The project's tip_supply.txt gives 1,248 held 300 uL tips for the starter against the CAD's 2,208 (flagged
    by the second check).

==============================================================================================================
WHERE EACH MACHINE IS CLEARLY STRONGER
==============================================================================================================
  Opentrons Flex over LH-1:
    It exists, ships, and has published, tested accuracy and precision for every pipette; LH-1 has none
    measured. It has pressure-based liquid detection (on the end channels), tip-attachment sensing, partial-
    column pick-up, a bought gripper, thermocycler, heater-shaker, temperature module, magnet, plate reader,
    stacker and HEPA/UV, each with stated specifications. It is smaller (870 x 690 x 840 mm) and lighter (88.5
    kg) than the LH-1 starter (1,456 x 894 x 1,190 mm, about 150 kg), carries IEC 61010 compliance, CE/ETL
    marks, a warranty and service contracts, and has open-source software and firmware under Apache-2.0. LH-1
    has no safety assessment, no licence chosen, and an unresolved tool-exchange mechanism.

  Hamilton STARlet over LH-1:
    Eight independent channels with independent spacing, 0.5-1,000 uL per channel (5 mL channels optional),
    published trueness and precision down to 0.5 uL, capacitive and pressure liquid-level detection, real-time
    pressure monitoring of every aspirate and dispense (TADM), clot and empty-well detection, and optional
    96/384 heads. Mature software with 21 CFR Part 11 tools, IVDR-compliant variants (SECONDARY), factory
    final-inspection pipetting results per instrument, field-service installation and remote condition
    monitoring. Comparable mass (135 kg) and a smaller footprint than LH-1. A large used market exists
    (SECONDARY).

  Opentrons Flex over Hamilton STARlet:
    Published list prices, lower mass (88.5 vs 135 kg) and footprint, Python API and open-source software and
    firmware, user-attachable pipettes, a 96-channel option sharing the same mounts, a bought plate reader,
    stacker and HEPA/UV module from the same maker.

  Hamilton STARlet over Opentrons Flex:
    Independent channels (any well pattern, variable spacing, tubes to plates), liquid sensing on every
    channel rather than end channels only, TADM monitoring, more deck capacity (30 tracks), regulated-use
    documentation.

  Where LH-1 would be stronger, IF the design works as calculated (none of this is demonstrated):
    Automatic exchange between six pipette types and a syringe dispense tool within one run (neither
    commercial machine exchanges pipettes automatically); a large on-deck tip supply refilled by the gripper
    (about 3,600 tips held in the starter, 6,100 in the large size); dedicated cooled places for plates,
    strips and tubes from 1.5 to 50 mL; per-dispense checks by camera and, in the large size, by weight; a
    design that is meant to be published for others to build and that grows in place from S to L. Cost cannot
    be compared: no costed bill of materials exists for the current design.

  Plain summary:
    Today the comparison is between two working, measured, supported products and a design on paper. The
    commercial machines are stronger on every property that depends on existing: measured performance, safety
    assessment, support, documentation and availability. LH-1's claimed advantages are design intentions until
    the bench-rig tests (latch release and re-latch, coupling repeatability, exchange reliability, pipetting
    accuracy after exchange) have passed.
