5. SAFETY ARRANGEMENT AND WHY
DOOR, fitted (not an option).  system.yaml safety_chain / stop_category_claim give MOTION exactly two energy removals, the e-stop (SR1 + KM1/KM2) and the door (SR2 + KM3/KM4), designed toward PL d Cat 3, and no reduced-speed or open-frame mode; the MVP CAD (cad/lh1/machine_family/out/MVP report.json guarding) keeps the starter's full enclosure with the coded door sensor, e-stop and reset because the MVP has the same moving axes.  So the MVP's safety wiring is the starter's, unchanged.
The SR1 feedback loop keeps its starter wiring: the mirror contacts of the absent KM5, TC_RELAY, TB_RELAY and KM6 are replaced by feed-through bridge
terminals in their rail positions (they monitor contactors that do not exist); each is removed when its contactor is fitted. SR1 output 33-34 is unused.
The all-tools-up chain runs through lever-splice bridges at the empty Z_UP_B and Z_UP_GRIP positions (axes that do not exist are 'up').
